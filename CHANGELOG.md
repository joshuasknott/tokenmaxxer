## TokenMaxxer 0.1.0 - 2026-09-01

The first public preview of TokenMaxxer.

- Track usage across Codex, Antigravity, DeepSeek, Z.ai, OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Cursor, xAI, Bedrock, Azure OpenAI, Fireworks, and Contextual AI accounts in one place
- See remaining quota, reset windows, and account balances at a glance
- View estimated spend and token history per account
- Drill into any account for model-level breakdowns
- Credentials stay in your operating system's secure storage; TokenMaxxer has no hosted account or telemetry service
- Unsigned community preview builds are available for Windows, macOS, and Linux, with operating system security prompts expected
